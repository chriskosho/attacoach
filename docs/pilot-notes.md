# AttaCoach pilot: note for coaches

Hi, thanks for trying AttaCoach. It is a free practice planner for youth baseball and softball coaches, ages 4U to 14U. Tell it your team, your age group and what the team needs to work on, and it builds a practice plan around that. It also runs the practice on a timer on your phone and learns from what you log afterwards.

**What it is:** a web app, not an app-store download. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your teams, plans and log stay on your phone's browser. It works without signal once it has loaded.

**Add it to your home screen**
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then tap Add to Home Screen.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

It then opens like an app from your home screen.

**Save a backup.** Your data lives on this phone. Settings, then Backup, then Save backup, saves a file you keep yourself (in Downloads, or Save to Files on iPhone). Use Restore to bring it back on a new phone. Clearing your browser data deletes everything, so save a backup now and then.

**Send feedback.** Two ways, both by email:
- Settings, then Send feedback.
- On any plan, tap "Was this plan right?" (Yes or Not quite), with an optional note.

Each one shows you the exact message before anything happens. Tapping "Open my email app" opens a draft to me; nothing is sent until you press send there. Names of players, coaches and teams are never included. Please leave names out of notes too.

**What is not built yet**
- Syncing between phones or with a computer. Use a backup file to move your data.
- Accounts and sign-in. They are built but switched off for this pilot.
- Sharing one team's roster or plans directly with another coach. You can share a plan's text from the plan page.
- Pitch counting, stats, reminders or notifications.
- Videos of our own. The YouTube button opens a search for a drill. Each search was checked first, but the videos are not ours.

Thank you for the time. I'm learning from every note.

Chris

## For the owner only: what to look for in feedback emails

Do not send this part to coaches.

- **Plan did not match the team.** Wrong length, space or kids in the message, or drills the team could not run (for example, pitching for a team that does not pitch). Check the message's header lines first.
- **A drill that felt unsafe.** Anything about throwing at a face, a crowded station, a runner, heat, or a child left out of a station. Treat these as the first thing to read.
- **Timer problems.** The clock stopped, a block was skipped, the sound or vibration did not fire, or the screen went dark mid-practice.
- **Confusing screens.** A coach who did not know what to tap, or said "I thought it would…". Note the screen name in the message.
- **Repeats.** The same drill or closing game in several notes means one fix to the library or planner, not many.
- **Missing features.** Save for later, and do not promise anything in reply.
